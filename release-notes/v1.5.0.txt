v1.5.0

• NEW: Zoom into any part of the image — when zoomed in, areas near the edges and corners can now be dragged to the middle of the screen.
• NEW: Pan Range setting — choose how much of the image must stay on screen while you drag it (5%–50%).
• FIX: Pinch zoom no longer drifts away from your fingers, and pinching, moving and rotating now work together in one gesture.
• FIX: An image zoomed out smaller than the screen can now be moved.
